Good to know

Frequently asked

Do I need kayaking experience?

No. The lagoon is calm, shallow, and protected by the reef. You get a short briefing before you set off, and the routes are easy to follow.

What should I bring?

Sunscreen, a hat, water, and shoes that can get wet. We provide life jackets and a dry bag for your phone and valuables.

How do I pay?

You pay when you arrive — cash or card. Booking online just reserves your kayak and date.

What if the weather turns bad?

If conditions aren't safe to paddle, we contact you to rebook for another day at no cost, or cancel your booking entirely.

Can children join?

Children can share the tandem kayak with an adult. Message us on WhatsApp with ages and we'll recommend the best setup.
